What this is
With a catalog, MCP MinPriv_BIC proposes the intersection of observed and cataloged tools, reports catalog tools that were not observed, and separately reports observed names outside the catalog. Without a catalog, the proposed allowlist is observed-use only.
This is evidence from observed workloads, not proof that unused tools will never be needed. Review before enforcement.
